# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project should be respectful, constructive, and focused
on improving the software. Welcome people regardless of experience,
background, identity, or perspective. Give actionable feedback, assume good
faith, and respect privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information or document content without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any
other space used to represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact options on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing
personal or sensitive information.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful or disruptive.
Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as confidentially as
reasonably possible.
